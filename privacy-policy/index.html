1 Who We Are

CartPulse ("we", "us", "our") is a Shopify application that helps e-commerce merchants recover abandoned shopping carts through automated multi-channel notifications, including browser push notifications and email reminders.

This Privacy Policy explains how CartPulse collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you install and use the CartPulse app on your Shopify store, and when your customers interact with CartPulse's notification features.

For questions about this policy, contact us at apps.kaswebtech@gmail.com.

2 Information We Collect

We collect different types of information depending on whether you are a merchant (Shopify store owner) or an end customer (shopper at a merchant's store).

Information from Merchants:

Data Type What It Is Why We Collect It
Shop domain Your myshopify.com store URL To identify your store and deliver services
OAuth access tokens Shopify API authentication tokens To read checkout/order data from your store
App configuration Notification settings, email preferences To customise notifications per your preferences
Usage analytics Recovery rates, notification counts To display your dashboard analytics

Information from End Customers (via merchant's store):

Data Type What It Is Why We Collect It
Email address Customer's email from checkout To send cart recovery email reminders
Name Customer's first/last name if provided To personalise notification messages
Cart contents Product names, quantities, prices To show cart items in recovery emails
Cart total Value of abandoned cart To display in reminders and track recovery revenue
Checkout URL Link to customer's saved checkout To direct customers back to complete purchase
Push subscription Browser endpoint URL and encryption keys To deliver browser push notifications

We collect customer data only through Shopify's checkout webhooks and only for customers who have entered their email address during checkout at a merchant's store. We do not use cookies, trackers, or third-party pixel technologies for ad targeting.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Abandoned cart detection — Identifying when a customer starts but does not complete checkout
  • Sending recovery notifications — Delivering push notifications and emails at scheduled intervals (1 hour, 24 hours, 1 week)
  • Tracking recovery — Marking a cart as recovered when the corresponding order is placed
  • Analytics dashboard — Aggregating recovery rates and revenue data for the merchant's dashboard
  • App functionality — Authenticating merchant sessions, storing preferences, and scheduling jobs
  • Service improvement — Understanding how the app is used to improve features

We do not use customer data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than cart recovery for the specific merchant whose store triggered the event.

4 Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We share data only with the following service providers who help us operate CartPulse:

  • Shopify Inc. — Our app runs within the Shopify platform. Shopify processes payment and store data under their own Privacy Policy.
  • Email service provider (Gmail / SMTP) — Used to send cart recovery emails on behalf of merchants. Emails contain only the customer's own cart data.
  • Redis / Upstash — A job queue service used to schedule and deliver notifications at the correct times. Cart data is temporarily stored in the queue during scheduling.
  • Hosting infrastructure — Our servers are hosted on a managed hosting provider. All data at rest is stored within our controlled database environment.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights, property, or the safety of our users.

5 Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the CartPulse service:

  • Abandoned cart records — Retained while your store has CartPulse installed. Deleted upon app uninstall.
  • Notification logs — Retained for analytics purposes while the app is installed.
  • Push subscriptions — Retained until the customer's browser unsubscribes or the app is uninstalled.
  • Merchant OAuth sessions — Deleted immediately when the app is uninstalled via Shopify's uninstall webhook.
  • Job queue data — Temporary; jobs are removed from the queue once processed.

When a merchant uninstalls CartPulse, we receive a webhook notification from Shopify and automatically delete all associated session and store data from our systems within 48 hours.

6 Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the data we hold, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Shopify HMAC signature verification for all incoming webhooks
  • Access tokens stored securely in our database, not in client-side code
  • Push notification encryption keys handled per the Web Push Protocol specification
  • Access to production data limited to authorised personnel only

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7 Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — Request that we correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — Request that we delete your personal data
  • Portability — Request a portable copy of your data
  • Objection — Object to processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Opt-out of push notifications — End customers can unsubscribe from push notifications at any time through their browser settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at apps.kaswebtech@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Merchants: You can delete all your data and disconnect CartPulse by uninstalling the app from your Shopify admin. This triggers automatic deletion of all associated data.

8 Shopify Merchants

By installing CartPulse, you grant us the Shopify API scopes listed in our app listing (read_orders, read_checkouts, write_script_tags). We access your store's data only to the extent necessary to provide the cart recovery service.

You are responsible for ensuring that your use of CartPulse complies with applicable privacy laws in your jurisdiction and with Shopify's Partner Programme Agreement. If your store has customers in the EU or UK, you must ensure your store's privacy policy discloses that you use a third-party cart recovery service (CartPulse) that may process customer checkout data.

9 End Customers of Merchants

If you are a shopper at a Shopify store that uses CartPulse and you have received a cart recovery notification, your data was processed because you began a checkout on that merchant's store.

To opt out of push notifications: You can disable push notifications for any website through your browser settings (Settings → Notifications → Site Permissions).

To opt out of email reminders: Contact the merchant whose store you were shopping at, or reply to the recovery email requesting removal.

To request deletion of your data: Contact us at apps.kaswebtech@gmail.com with the store domain and your email address. We will delete your cart records within 30 days.

10 Children's Privacy

CartPulse is intended for use by Shopify merchants operating legitimate e-commerce businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11 Changes to This Policy

Continued use of CartPulse after the effective date of a revised policy constitutes acceptance of the updated terms. If you disagree with the changes, you may uninstall the app from your Shopify admin.
